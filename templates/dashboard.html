How attack chances are estimated

The percentage estimates whether a player will use at least one attack if selected for a regular war. It uses their last 10 confirmed, unexcused regular-war appearances. Unselected, live, incomplete and CWL results are excluded. Known departures and returns start a new prediction sample.

To avoid treating a small sample as certain, the estimate is (appearances with an attack + 1) ÷ (appearances + 2). For example, attacking in 8 of 10 appearances gives a 75% estimate. No confirmed appearances gives no estimate; fewer than 5 is labeled limited history.

This is a history-based estimate, not a calibrated prediction or a measure of attack quality. Approved excuses and final-count corrections update it. War preference is the last saved API value; it does not make the attack chance zero when someone opts out. Hover its label for the saved check time.

Start here

Your leadership workspace

Sign in with your approved leadership account. Legacy passphrase access remains available where configured. Use the navigation along the bottom to move between Home, Members, Wars, Capital and Management. The dashboard shows saved snapshots; the saved time tells you how recent they are.

Dates and times follow the display settings. Settings lets you choose a 12-hour or 24-hour clock, compact player cards and other display preferences.

1. Get a quick clan overview

Home shows the member count, attack use, bench and kick flags, weekly league points and growth. Select a summary card to see the players or results behind it.

2. Look up a player

Open Members to search by name or player tag and filter the roster. The little Town Hall next to each name shows their level. Select a player card for their war attendance, contributions, upgrades and captured profile information.

Use the report’s notes area to record leadership context. Hover over a graph point for details; move the pointer away to clear them. On a touch screen, tap a point and tap elsewhere to dismiss its details.

3. Review wars and plan a lineup

Wars contains the current saved war, past results and attack performance. The countdown uses the recorded war end time. A final-results refresh is scheduled for five minutes after the recorded end; the cloud scheduler can start it later.

Open the lineup planner, choose Regular or CWL, set the team size and Town Hall balance, then choose Suggest lineup. Review the selected players, reserves and availability warnings. Checkbox changes update a working draft. Commit lineup confirms a complete team; export the committed checklist when ready. You make the actual roster changes in Clash.

Confirmed missed attacks drive the bench rule. Unknown results are marked for review. Approved exceptions pause the streak. CWL is tracked separately.

4. Find recruiting candidates

Open Management → Recruiter. New this scan and Still available contain verified clanless candidates from the latest saved scan. Use search, Town Hall, war-star, league and status filters to narrow the list. Expand a candidate’s history to see verification times and their observed departure window.

Choose Open in Clash to check the current profile before inviting. Joined War Haven and No longer available keep recent recruiting outcomes separate from available candidates. Clan turnover shows which tracked clans produce candidates.

5. Refresh the data

Use the refresh button for the data you want to collect. An update continues in the cloud if you close the page. You can exit after the request is accepted; it continues in the cloud. If this page stays open, the new data appears automatically without signing you out.

Opening or reloading the page only loads the latest published snapshot. It does not collect fresh game data.

6. Save and protect your work

Use the saving status to confirm whether changes are shared, waiting to sync or kept locally. A lineup draft is separate from the committed lineup.

Export private backup makes an optional readable copy. Keep it somewhere private. Import backup combines history and edits with the current data.

Leadership display preferences are shared across leaders. Personal player display settings belong to that login and village. Sign out this device when you finish on a shared computer.

Your regular-war rule

01
First 0/2 appearance

Bench the next regular war, then return. Miss war 1, sit out war 2, return in war 3.

02
Second consecutive 0/2 appearance

Flag for a kick after war 3. You make the roster changes and kicks in Clash.

03
Missed both, participated, then missed both again

Show a Possible kick warning under Kick flags when a player misses both attacks, uses at least one attack in a later assigned regular war, then misses both again. Leadership reviews the pattern and makes the decision in Clash.

Benched wars and approved exceptions pause the streak. Using at least one attack resets the consecutive-miss streak. Possible-kick warnings stay until leadership reviews the history. Missing one attack has no automatic penalty. CWL is tracked separately without an automatic penalty. Only confirmed final results create attendance flags. These rules carry across seasons.

Keep results up to date

Automatic updates refresh the saved clan data. Use the refresh button for a new capture and wait for its completion message.

Final war results are confirmed after the API reports the war has ended and a complete capture is available. An update can arrive late. Missing or incomplete results never create an automatic missed-attack penalty.

Opening the dashboard shows the latest saved capture. The owner can review successful updates, failures and data freshness in Management → Operations.
